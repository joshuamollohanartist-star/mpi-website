What MPI Is

Distribution is built in.
The system is the product.

Most artists have access to distribution. What they lack is a structured release process, campaign discipline, and tools built for a specific lane.

MPI is built from real release volume, not theory. Every framework, every automation, every scoring metric was forged inside an active catalog — 165+ songs, 52 albums written and produced, one release every Friday without fail.

This isn't a distribution upload button with a dashboard tacked on. It's an operating system for artists who treat releasing as a discipline.

The Process

How to apply.

Applications are reviewed on five criteria. You'll know exactly where you stand.

  1. 01

    Submit your application

    Takes 10–15 minutes. You'll need your Spotify profile link, release history, and a description of your current release process.

  2. 02

    We score you on 5 criteria

    Platform momentum, creative quality, release consistency, marketing effort, and brand alignment. Every application is reviewed by a human. No auto-rejections.

  3. 03

    You hear back within 14 business days

    Accepted applications move to onboarding. Declined applications receive a full scorecard — not a form rejection. You'll know exactly what needs to change.

  4. 04

    Onboard and run your first release cycle

    Your first 6-week release cycle begins. The system is pre-loaded. The calendar is set. You execute.
