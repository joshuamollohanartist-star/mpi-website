Section 4

Fan Hub Revenue Share Terms

4.1 Fan Hub Overview

The MPI Fan Hub is an optional feature available on eligible subscription tiers that allows artists to offer fan subscriptions, exclusive content, or other paid offerings directly to their audience. All fan payments processed through the Fan Hub are handled via PayPal.

4.2 MPI Platform Commission

For all revenue generated through the Fan Hub, MPI retains a platform commission of 10% of net revenue. "Net revenue" is defined as gross fan payments received, less PayPal transaction fees. PayPal's standard transaction fee for the Fan Hub is currently 3.49% of the transaction amount plus $0.49 per transaction, though PayPal may update its fee schedule independently of MPI. You acknowledge and agree that the applicable PayPal fee will be deducted before MPI's 10% commission is calculated.

Example: If a fan pays $10.00, and PayPal fees are $0.84 (3.49% × $10.00 + $0.49), the net revenue is $9.16. MPI retains $0.916 (10%), and the artist receives $8.244.

MPI reserves the right to adjust its commission rate with 30 days' advance notice to affected artists via email and platform notification. Continued use of the Fan Hub after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the revised rate.

4.3 Payouts

Artist Fan Hub earnings (after MPI commission and PayPal fees) will be remitted to the artist's designated PayPal account on or before the last calendar day of the month following the month in which the fan payments were received. For example, fan payments received in May will be paid out on or before June 30.

Payouts are contingent on your providing a valid PayPal account and completing any identity verification required by MPI or PayPal. MPI may delay or withhold payouts in cases of suspected fraud, chargebacks, policy violations, or as required by applicable law. Minimum payout thresholds may apply; if your balance does not meet the minimum at the end of a payout period, it will roll over to the next month.

4.4 Artist Responsibilities

You are solely responsible for the content you offer through the Fan Hub, for complying with all applicable laws in connection with fan transactions (including tax obligations), and for honoring any commitments you make to fans. MPI is a platform intermediary and is not responsible for fulfilling artist obligations to fans.
